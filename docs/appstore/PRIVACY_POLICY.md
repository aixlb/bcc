## 中文

**包拆拆（XLB Storyboard）** 是一款在你本机运行的视频拆解工具。我们高度重视你的隐私。

### 1. 我们收集的数据
**我们（开发者）不收集任何个人数据。** 应用不含分析、埋点或广告 SDK，不会把你的任何数据上传到开发者的服务器（我们也没有服务器接收这些数据）。

### 2. 数据存放在哪里
- 你的 **API Key**、导入的视频、生成的剧本/分镜表/风格指南等**全部仅保存在你本机**的应用沙盒目录中（macOS 容器目录）。
- 删除应用或在应用内删除项目即可移除相应数据。

### 3. 第三方 AI 服务
- 应用本身不内置、不代付任何 AI 服务。要进行分析，你需自备并配置所选服务商（如 Google Gemini、Kimi 或你自定义的兼容接口）的 API Key。
- **仅当你主动发起分析时**，应用会把相应的视频帧/片段以及必要的文本，**从你的设备直接发送到你所配置的那家 AI 服务商**进行处理。这些数据**不经过开发者**。
- 这些数据如何被处理、保留，取决于你所选服务商的隐私政策，请参阅对应服务商的条款（例如 Google、Moonshot/Kimi 等）。

### 4. 儿童
本应用面向一般用户，不针对儿童，也不会有意收集儿童数据。

### 5. 变更
本政策如有更新会修改顶部日期。

### 6. 联系
如有疑问，请通过应用的支持页面（Support URL）联系我们。

## English

**XLB Storyboard** is a video-breakdown tool that runs locally on your Mac. We take your privacy seriously.

### 1. Data we collect
**We (the developer) do not collect any personal data.** The app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, and does not upload any of your data to developer servers (we operate no server that receives such data).

### 2. Where your data is stored
- Your **API key**, imported videos, and generated scripts/storyboards/style guides are stored **only on your device**, inside the app's macOS sandbox container.
- Deleting the app, or deleting a project inside the app, removes the corresponding data.

### 3. Third-party AI services
- The app includes no built-in or paid AI service. To run an analysis you must provide and configure your own API key for a provider you choose (e.g. Google Gemini, Kimi, or a custom compatible endpoint).
- **Only when you start an analysis** does the app send the relevant video frames/segments and necessary text **directly from your device to the AI provider you configured**. This data **does not pass through the developer**.
- How that data is processed and retained is governed by your chosen provider's privacy policy; please review their terms (e.g. Google, Moonshot/Kimi).

### 4. Children
The app is intended for general audiences, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect children's data.

### 5. Changes
Updates to this policy are reflected by the date at the top.

### 6. Contact
For questions, reach us via the app's Support URL.
