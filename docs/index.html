隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-06-19

中文

包拆拆（XLB Storyboard）是一款在你本机运行的视频拆解工具。我们高度重视你的隐私。

1. 我们收集的数据：我们（开发者）不收集任何个人数据。应用不含分析、埋点或广告 SDK，不会把你的任何数据上传到开发者的服务器。

2. 数据存放：你的 API Key、导入的视频、生成的剧本/分镜表/风格指南全部仅保存在你本机的应用沙盒目录中。删除应用或在应用内删除项目即可移除。

3. 第三方 AI 服务：应用本身不内置、不代付任何 AI 服务。要进行分析，你需自备并配置所选服务商（如 Google Gemini、Kimi 或自定义兼容接口）的 API Key。仅当你主动发起分析时，应用会把相应的视频帧/片段及必要文本从你的设备直接发送到你所配置的那家 AI 服务商，这些数据不经过开发者。其处理与保留遵循对应服务商的隐私政策。

4. 儿童：本应用面向一般用户，不针对儿童，也不会有意收集儿童数据。

5. 联系：如有疑问，请通过上方支持渠道联系我们。

English

XLB Storyboard runs locally on your Mac. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Data we collect: We (the developer) do not collect any personal data. No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs; nothing is uploaded to developer servers.

2. Storage: Your API key, imported videos, and generated scripts/storyboards/style guides are stored only on your device, in the app's macOS sandbox container. Deleting the app or a project removes the corresponding data.

3. Third-party AI: The app includes no built-in or paid AI service. You provide your own API key for a provider you choose. Only when you start an analysis does the app send the relevant video frames/segments and text directly from your device to the provider you configured; this data does not pass through the developer. Processing is governed by that provider's privacy policy.

4. Children: The app is for general audiences and does not knowingly collect children's data.

5. Contact: For questions, reach us via the support links above.
